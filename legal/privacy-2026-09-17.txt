Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use Google Analytics to understand visits to our public website and interactions with its links. Analytics measurement is enabled when you accept analytics in our consent controls. You can reject or withdraw consent through “Analytics preferences” in the footer. The Google tag script is requested when a page loads; analytics measurement remains disabled until acceptance. Advertising consent remains denied in this website configuration.

Our custom analytics events do not include names, email addresses, application contents, booking answers or skin-analysis results. Page addresses supplied by this website’s tracking code exclude query strings and unapproved route identifiers. A booking-link click measures interest and does not confirm that an appointment was made. Information about Google’s data handling is available in Google’s Privacy Policy.
